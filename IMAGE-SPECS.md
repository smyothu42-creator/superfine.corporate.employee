# Photo Specs

Two photo slots in the app. Here is what to export for each.

---

## 1. Meal card photo

The square thumbnail on every meal card.

**Export: 400 × 400 px — square (1:1)**

| Where it shows | Size on screen |
| --- | --- |
| Mobile | 96 × 96 |
| Tablet & desktop | 112 × 112 |

**Keep in mind**

- Always a square. Corners are rounded 12px.
- A round **"+" button overlaps the bottom-right corner.** Keep that corner
  clear — no plating detail or garnish you care about.
- A small red count badge can appear in the **top-left** corner.
- Center the food. The photo is cropped from the centre outward.

---

## 2. Promotion banner photo

The photo on the right side of each promo card at the top of the menu.

**Export: 800 × 800 px — square (1:1), food centred**

| Where it shows | Size on screen |
| --- | --- |
| Mobile | 135 × 161 (taller than wide) |
| Tablet | 134 × 172 |
| Laptop | 179 × 172 |
| Desktop | 220 × 172 (largest it ever gets) |

**Keep in mind**

- **The shape changes between phone and desktop.** On a phone the slot is
  *portrait*; on desktop it is *landscape*. The same file is used for both, so
  a wide landscape photo will lose its left and right edges on mobile.
  A square export with the food in the middle works everywhere.
- **The left edge fades out.** A soft gradient covers roughly the left 56px so
  the photo blends into the card. Nothing important goes near the left edge.
- The photo never appears larger than 220px wide, so there is no need to export
  anything bigger than 800px.

---

## Both photos

- **Format:** JPG for photos, quality ~80. WebP is fine if available.
- **File size:** aim under 150 KB each.
- **Crop:** centre crop, always. The app never letterboxes or squashes — it
  fills the frame and trims the overflow.
- **Background:** real food photography on a plain surface reads best. Busy
  backgrounds get lost at 96px.
- **If a photo is missing** or fails to load, the app shows a branded yellow
  tile with a utensils icon in its place. Nothing shifts or breaks.
